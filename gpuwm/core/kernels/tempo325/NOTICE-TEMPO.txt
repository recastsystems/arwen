Modifications: translated from Fortran to C++/CUDA for WOOF; all
parameters and lookup tables are read from a flat float64 blob; Fortran
optional arguments became nullable pointers; the module SAVE flag
first_call_main carries tempo_run's is_first_step and is supplied by the
caller; where v3.2.5 reads the local final_weight in sum_tendencies without
setting it (main.F90:1752-1755) this port defines it as 1 (no scaling).
